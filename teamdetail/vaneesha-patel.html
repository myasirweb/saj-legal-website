Biography

Vaneesha is a dedicated and results-driven legal professional with a strong background in litigation, personal injury and immigration law. Coming from a diverse cultural background, with multilingual proficiency in English, Bahasa Melayu, Gujarati, Hindi, Punjabi and Urdu, along with conversational fluency in Tamil, she is able to connect with clients from various backgrounds and navigate complex legal matters with cultural sensitivity. Called to the Bar of England & Wales with a Distinction classification, Vaneesha has developed extensive experience in managing legal disputes, drafting compelling pleadings and negotiating high-value settlements. She has successfully handled Small Claims, Fast Track and Multi-Track cases, achieving a high success rate in mediation and trial proceedings. Her expertise extends to judicial reviews and litigation against public bodies, where she has played a pivotal role in securing significant settlements and advancing human rights claims.
